What to Expect

A Typical Class at CV Chess Club

Every session is structured, engaging, and built around the individual player — not a one-size-fits-all curriculum.

01

Warm-Up Puzzles

Students start with tactical puzzles to sharpen pattern recognition and get their minds in the game.

02

Coached Instruction

Coach JB works directly with students on openings, strategy, and specific skills appropriate to their level.

03

Tournament Play

Students play full games against each other with chess clocks — building real competitive experience.

04

Game Review

Coach JB reviews key moments from games, helping students understand their moves and improve.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

My child has never played chess. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Most of our students start with zero experience. Coach JB meets every child where they are. As one parent told us: "Even as a total beginner, my child never felt lost."

What is the monthly cost?

Monthly pricing is available upon registration. The same monthly rate applies regardless of how many class dates fall in that month. Contact us at chessclubcv@gmail.com or call (831) 392-7456 for current pricing.

What if we miss a class?

Families may attend another available session during the same month if space allows. No refunds are issued for missed classes. We follow the CUSD school calendar so you'll always know in advance when we're closed.

How many kids are in each class?

Every class is capped at 8 students. This isn't just a policy — it's a commitment. Small groups mean Coach JB can work with every child individually, not just manage a room.

What is the Competitive League add-on?

The Thursday Competitive League is for students who want to develop tournament skills. It's available as an add-on to any Recreational League session and focuses on tournament prep, advanced tactics, and rated play development.

How do I enroll?

Click the Enroll Now button on this page to fill out our registration form. Once approved, you'll receive a Stripe invoice due within 3 days. Payment secures your child's spot.

How do I cancel?

Send a written request to chessclubcv@gmail.com. We ask for as much notice as possible to allow other families on the waitlist to take the spot.

Ready to Make Your First Move?

Spots are limited to 8 per class. Enroll today to secure your child's seat.
