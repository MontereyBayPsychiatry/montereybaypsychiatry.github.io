TMS Therapy at
Monterey Bay Psychiatry

We are adding Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) therapy — an FDA-cleared, non-medication treatment for depression and OCD. TMS uses focused magnetic pulses to stimulate specific areas of the brain involved in mood regulation.

TMS is typically considered when patients have not responded adequately to one or more antidepressant medications. It is non-invasive, performed in-office, and does not require sedation or anesthesia.

Who TMS may be appropriate for:

  • ✓ Adults with Major Depressive Disorder who haven't responded to medication
  • ✓ Patients seeking a non-medication treatment option
  • ✓ OCD (Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder) — FDA-cleared indication

TMS availability date will be announced to existing patients first. If you'd like to be notified when TMS becomes available, reach out through our contact form.
