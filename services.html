Conditions We Treat

Expert care for the conditions that affect your daily life

We treat a focused, well-defined range of conditions — so you receive care from a provider with deep expertise, not a generalist stretched across everything.

Conditions Treated

  • ✓ ADHD — children, teens, and adults; comprehensive evaluation and ongoing treatment
  • ✓ Anxiety disorders — generalized anxiety, panic disorder, social anxiety, school refusal
  • ✓ Depression & mood disorders — MDD, dysthymia, bipolar II, cyclothymia
  • ✓ Trauma & PTSD — including crime victims and complex trauma
  • ✓ Perinatal mental health — pregnancy and postpartum depression and anxiety
  • ✓ Child & adolescent psychiatry — ages 5 and up; dual-certified pediatric expertise
  • ✓ College student mental health — academic stress, adjustment, anxiety, ADHD
  • ✓ Occupational burnout — high-functioning professionals experiencing burnout and overwhelm

Outside Our Scope

To ensure patient safety and provide the highest level of care, we refer the following to more specialized settings:

  • — Active psychosis or first-break psychosis requiring intensive support
  • — Active substance use disorders requiring detox or intensive outpatient treatment
  • — Eating disorders as a primary diagnosis
  • — Court-ordered psychiatric evaluations or legal/forensic reports
  • — Active suicidal ideation with plan or intent requiring higher level of care
Not sure if we're the right fit? Reach out — we're happy to help you find the right level of care.

Transparent Pricing

What to expect to pay

We are currently a cash-pay practice with insurance credentialing in progress. All fees are due at the time of service. Superbills provided upon request for out-of-network reimbursement.

Service Duration Price
Psychiatric Evaluation Comprehensive initial assessment — new patients only 60 min $350
ADHD Evaluation Comprehensive evaluation including psychiatric assessment, validated rating scales, diagnostic formulation, and treatment planning 2–3 hrs $750–$1,000
Follow-Up Appointment (60 min) Extended follow-up with medication management and integrated psychotherapy 60 min $325
Follow-Up Appointment (30 min) Standard follow-up for medication management and treatment review 30 min $225
Brief Follow-Up Brief medication check — established patients with stable treatment only 15–20 min $175
Insurance: We are actively credentialing with major commercial insurers and will begin accepting insurance plans once complete. A superbill (itemized receipt) is available upon request so you can seek out-of-network reimbursement from your insurer.
Cancellation policy: We require 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule appointments. Late cancellations and no-shows are subject to a fee. Your appointment time is reserved specifically for you — please reach out as soon as possible if you need to make changes.
